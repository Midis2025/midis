The people behind the work

"Together, we are more than a team; we are a family of thinkers, creators, and problem-solvers, united by a vision to drive digital growth."

Sagar Bakshi

MD

Co-Founder & Director Sagar Bakshi is the Managing Director of Midis. His journey began with a simple belief—that digital tools should make business growth easier, not more complicated. Over the years, he has worked with a wide range of clients, from agile startups to established brands, learning that every project requires its own unique approach. Sometimes it’s building a clean, functional website. Other times it’s designing campaigns that reach the right audience. And often, it’s guiding a team through the day-to-day challenges of growing in the digital space. Sagar also brings strong expertise in eCommerce, particularly with Shopify and Amazon, where he enjoys helping businesses scale steadily and sustainably. His approach has always been practical, creative, and results-driven. That same mindset continues to shape how Midis takes on new projects today, ensuring every solution is built to support real growth and long-term success. As a leader, Sagar believes in collaboration, transparency, and empowering his team—values that define both his leadership and the culture of Midis.

With extensive experience in strategy and digital solutions, he has played a key role in shaping Midis into a trusted partner for brands seeking more than just an online presence. From branding and advertising to eCommerce, his goal is simple yet impactful—to help businesses succeed while raising the bar for quality and innovation with every project.
